# Topic Selection

## Purpose

This document defines how documentary topics are selected.

---

## A topic should:

- Be interesting.
- Be based on real events.
- Have reliable information available.
- Be easy to understand.
- Have strong visual potential.

---

## Avoid topics that:

- Have very little information.
- Depend heavily on opinions.
- Cannot be visualized.
- Are repetitive.

---

## Selection Checklist

Before approving a topic, verify:

- Documentary value ✔

- Viewer interest ✔

- Enough available information ✔

- Visual storytelling potential ✔

- Suitable for ORVIO style ✔
